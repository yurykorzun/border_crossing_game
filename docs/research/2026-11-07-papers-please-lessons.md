# What worked in Papers, Please and similar games (2026-11-07)

TL;DR: Papers, Please works through escalating rules, tactile objects, very little text and a family left vague enough to project onto. Its weak spots are tedium and a fixed story schedule. Its 2022 portrait phone port is the closest thing to our format, and it lost the "see everything at once" overview. We only have ~6 encounters, so we can't lean on volume the way Papers, Please does. Costs in different currencies and a director that reacts to answers carry more weight for us.

No primary GDC postmortem found. Sources are Pope's devlogs and interviews. [unverified] marks fan-wiki or snippet-only claims.

## Papers, Please

- Rules escalate per day. Pope never introduced a new rule on a morning with a story encounter, because players ignored the bulletin ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-bleak-genius-of-i-papers-please-i-), [GDC 2015 narrative review](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2015/GameNarrativeReview/Skaggs_PapersPlease.pdf)).
- Drag-and-drop replaced click-to-sort because clicking "felt a bit lifeless" ([devlog](https://dukope.com/devlogs/papers-please/tig-00)).
- The family screen is "just a few dots with some text", left unspecified so players project their own family ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-bleak-genius-of-i-papers-please-i-)).
- About 2-3 scripted entrants a day, the rest generated within the day's rules. Dilemmas are short and tied to one person.
- Pope wanted more randomness in scheduling but the dependencies were too fragile. Replays lose discovery because story characters always appear in the same place.
- Cut: baggage search, UV seals, gunfights, a "supervisor watching" light that people clicked like a button ([Kill Screen](https://www.killscreen.com/peek-behind-scenes-papers-please-reveals-baggage-search-and-gun-fights/)).
- Tone: reduce dialogue, keep things ambiguous, leave some unresolved. Fictional country, messages general, some levity. "Don't tell or show something when you can make the player do it themselves" ([Reason](https://reason.com/2013/09/26/papers-please-politics-in-games-and-the/printer/)).
- Sparse sound, the stamp thud is what people remember.
- Disliked: tedium. iPad port (2014) mostly praised for touch. The 2022 phone port is portrait, a document carousel and a stamp drawer. It lost the overview of all documents and made comparing across swipes hard ([devlog](https://dukope.com/devlogs/papers-please/mobile)).

## Similar games, one lesson each

- Reigns: filter cards by state, weight the rest. Once players notice cards react to earlier choices, "every card becomes meaningful" ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-creating-an-adaptive-narrative-in-i-reigns-i-)).
- This War of Mine: civilian struggle first, death as valid an ending as survival ([GDC](https://gdcvault.com/play/1022335/This-War-of-Mine-Raising)).
- Beholder: a moral choice is a choice of two evils, priced in two currencies ([Thumbsticks](https://www.thumbsticks.com/warm-lamp-games-interview/)).
- Orwell: testers called it linear because they couldn't see the other options ([deep dive](https://www.jikguard.com/news/game-design-deep-dive-decisions-that-matter-in-orwell)).
- Suzerain: political choices land on the protagonist's marriage.
- Obra Dinn: confirms in batches of three to stop brute-force guessing.
- Bury Me, My Love: empathy through helplessness and waiting.

## Rules for Purpose of Visit

1. One new document or rule per checkpoint, introduced on the routine card (first), with the story card second. Never both in one beat.
2. "On file" and "What you said" on one screen, side by side, never a carousel.
3. Every checkable claim gets a visible "Logged" mark when made.
4. The stamp is physical: one strong sound plus haptic, other audio sparse.
5. Write little: 1-2 sentences per official line, leave outcomes unresolved.
6. Keep the father vague enough to project onto.
7. Fictional country, general regime, one light or absurd routine beat for levity.
8. Director reacts to answers and shuffles where story beats appear (avoids Pope's fixed schedule).
9. Every approach is a choice between costs in different currencies (evidence, time, a person, scrutiny).
10. Waiting room: keep it to 2-3 beats, and make the time it costs matter later, so the fixed result reads as helplessness and not as a fake option.
11. Endings are progress: show endings found and one hint toward a new one.
